## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Give every candidate an append-only review history: accept, correct, reject, restore and reopen become events, corrections become linked revisions, and Rust projects current state, eligibility and evidence validity on every read.

Purpose: KNOW-03/04 and the one-way D-12 contract; the eligibility read is what later phases will trust (KNOW-05, KNOW-08 invalidation).
Output: Reviews migration with a projection view, `review.rs`, projection/eligibility/history/runs/deletion-impact reads, and six Tauri commands.
</objective>
