## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Provide the Rust projection behind Today and Review: each recorded meeting with its Telu state and review counts, plus evidence-aware search.

Purpose: D-03 and D-07 need real data (no fake tabs or placeholder cards); canonical meetings must be findable even though they only have evidence rows.
Output: `agenda` module with three commands, evidence-aware search, and a typed TS service.
</objective>
