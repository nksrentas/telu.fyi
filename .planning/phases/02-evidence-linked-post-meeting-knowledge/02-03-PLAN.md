## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Harden the tracer's extraction path into the full D-15/D-16 evidence rules and a production run lifecycle: Unicode-safe verification, minimum quote length, cross-boundary quotes, stated-only owner and due, tolerant typed parsing, context-sized chunking, cancellation and crash recovery.

Purpose: The tracer proved the path; this makes every shown candidate defensible and every run terminate truthfully (KNOW-01, KNOW-02, KNOW-06).
Output: `prompt.rs`, `parse.rs`, a complete `span_verifier.rs`, chunked and cancellable orchestration, and a `knowledge_cancel_extraction` command.
</objective>
