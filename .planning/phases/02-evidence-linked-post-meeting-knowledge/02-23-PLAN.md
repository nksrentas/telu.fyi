## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Put each candidate's exact evidence and correction history one click away: the SourceChip, the EvidencePopover whose Open transcript centres and marks the cited words in the drawer, and the HistoryPopover for corrected items.

Purpose: KNOW-02 (exact transcript evidence in context), KNOW-04 (inspectable revisions linked to the original proposal and evidence) and KNOW-08 (changed or missing evidence is visible) need these surfaces; 02-24 places them in every candidate row.
Output: `SourceChip`, `EvidencePopover`, `HistoryPopover`, `EvidenceNavigationContext` in `meeting-page.tsx`, and their tests. Split from the original 02-15.
</objective>
