## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Let the user decide every candidate: the ReviewHead with the abstention line, CandidateRows with accept, correct, reject and restore and immediate Rust-reported eligibility, the collapsed Rejected group, Accept all and Undo — each row carrying its SourceChip, evidence popover and history.

Purpose: KNOW-03 (independent accept/correct/reject with visible eligibility), KNOW-04 (corrections as revisions), KNOW-05 (rejected items stay out unless restored), KNOW-06 (abstention and unresolved fields visible) and KNOW-08 (invalidated evidence visible) become controllable here.
Output: `ReviewHead`, `CandidateRow`, `RejectedGroup`, the review block in `KnowledgeSection`, tests and gallery screens. Split from the original 02-15; builds on the run lifecycle (02-15) and the evidence surfaces (02-23).
</objective>
