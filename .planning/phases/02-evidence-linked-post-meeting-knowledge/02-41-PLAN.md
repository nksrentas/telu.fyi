## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Second extraction lever for the KNOW-07 gap (02-VERIFICATION.md, SC5): the precision checks. In 02-37, cited quotes were exact spans (51/51) but often did not support the whole claim (semantic support 29/51), and claims were often untrue to the meeting (39/51; questions 1/5). This plan adds two model checks per window on top of the v3 base from 02-40: a support check against the verified quotes, and a follow-up check against what the meeting says later.

Purpose: raise semantic support, claim correctness and review acceptance without touching the deterministic guarantees (exact spans, owner/due rules, matcher, invariants).
Output: support.rs, followup.rs, their integration in window_candidates, and a labeled development measurement.
</objective>
