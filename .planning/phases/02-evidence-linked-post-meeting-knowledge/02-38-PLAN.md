## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Close the measurement half of the KNOW-07 gap (02-VERIFICATION.md, SC5: representative gate fails on semantic support, claim correctness, evidence coverage, commitment attribution and review acceptance). Before extraction can be improved, a maintainer needs to measure it on the 10 spent meetings without the GUI, reproducibly, with the exact pipeline Find knowledge runs.

Purpose: every later lever plan (02-40, 02-41) and the development gate (02-42) depend on this measurement. It must measure what the app runs, so it reuses the production completer and window budget and the existing replay functions, and it must never be able to stand in for the representative evaluation.
Output: `telu-knowledge-eval live`, `run --budget-tokens`, `score --review-plan` with `evaluationKind: development`, the development set in `$HOME/telu-know07-dev/fixtures`, and a calibrated baseline measurement recorded in the SUMMARY. The Dev measurement procedure and the Blind labeling brief at the end of this plan are the fixed procedure every later plan reuses.
</objective>

## Blind labeling brief (verbatim, identical for every development measurement and for the 02-43 held-out evaluation)

> You are labeling knowledge items that a meeting tool extracted from one meeting transcript. You get one JSON file with the meeting's transcript segments (id, start, end, text), its gold items (goldId, type, text, evidenceSegmentIds, owner, due) and the predicted items (predId, type, text, owner, due, links with quotes). Judge each predicted item on its own, with the transcript as the only source of truth. For every predId write:
> - matchesGoldId: the goldId of the gold item this item expresses (same type, same content), or null.
> - claimCorrect: true only if the item is true to the meeting. A decision was actually agreed, not a proposal, option, "could", "maybe" or condition, and not a recap that is changed later in the meeting. A commitment was actually taken on by someone. A question was asked and is not answered anywhere later in the meeting. A fact was stated and is not contradicted later.
> - quoteSupportsClaim: true only if the quoted words alone support everything the item text asserts. Judge this separately from claimCorrect.
> - ownerCorrect and dueCorrect, for every item of any type: true if the shown owner or due matches what the words state, or if it shows "not stated" and the words state none; false otherwise.
> Then decide a review action per predId: "accept" when claimCorrect, quoteSupportsClaim, ownerCorrect and dueCorrect are all true; "correct" when claimCorrect is true but the wording claims more than the quotes support, or the owner or due is wrong or missing; "reject" otherwise. For "correct", also give the corrected text, owner and due.
> Output two JSON objects: labels as {predId: {matchesGoldId, claimCorrect, quoteSupportsClaim, ownerCorrect, dueCorrect}} and the review plan as {predId: {action, text, owner, due}}. You are not told how the labels will be scored, and you do not open any other file.

These rules restate the 02-35 and 02-37 labeling rules (claim correctness and quote support judged separately; accept, correct or reject) and make explicit the reasons the 02-37 labeler recorded (proposals, superseded recaps, answered questions, missing owners).
