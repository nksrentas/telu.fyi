## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Move recording into the shell (D-04): Record and the recording capsule live in the toolbars, recording state and recovery are hosted once in the layout, and the meeting page shows the live transcript — with every Phase 1 recording guarantee intact.

Purpose: Meetings that become knowledge are created here; the old home page and floating control bar are replaced.
Output: `use-recording-controls`, RecordingHost, RecordSlot/capsule, LiveTranscript; RecordingControls and the home TranscriptPanel removed; Phase 1 contract tests re-pointed.
</objective>
