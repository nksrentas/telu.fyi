## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Declare the KNOW-07 quality thresholds before evaluation (user decision), commit them, and produce the first scored, content-free report — keeping the exit gate honest about what is and is not yet measured, with a derived KNOW-07 status that stays open until thresholds are declared and the representative-meeting evaluation passes.

Purpose: The roadmap forbids invented numeric targets; Phase 3 depends on predeclared, passing thresholds on representative meetings. Undeclared thresholds or a pending representative evaluation mean KNOW-07 is not yet satisfied.
Output: A pilot metrics report, the user-declared `thresholds-v1.json` (own commit), a synthetic scored report, `eval/know-07-status.json` (open when this plan finishes) with its consistency test, and the representative-evaluation procedure as an end-of-phase human check.
</objective>
