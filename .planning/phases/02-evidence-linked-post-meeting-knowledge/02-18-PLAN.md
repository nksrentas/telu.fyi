## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Give maintainers an offline, content-free evaluation harness that measures extraction by item type — claim correctness, exact-span support, evidence coverage, attribution, duplication, abstention and review outcome — against thresholds recorded before scoring.

Purpose: KNOW-07 and the phase exit gate; Phase 3 may proceed only on predeclared, measured results.
Output: `telu-knowledge-eval` (score, run, export), shared pure extraction pipeline, synthetic fixtures and CLI tests.
</objective>
