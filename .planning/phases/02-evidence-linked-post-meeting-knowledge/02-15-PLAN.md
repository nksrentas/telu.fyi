## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Make every Knowledge run on the meeting page visible and truthful before and after it happens: the provider label first, validated starts, the D-09 auto-run gate, every run state, regenerate (D-11) and run details — plus the knowledge-service wrappers the review and evidence plans consume.

Purpose: KNOW-01 (derive candidates from a completed meeting), KNOW-05 (regenerate never resurrects decided items; counts shown) and KNOW-06 (abstention is visible at run level) become user-controllable here, with Phase 1 D-18/D-19 privacy guarantees on every start.
Output: `RunNotice`, `RunDetailsPopover`, the run-lifecycle `KnowledgeSection`, the extended knowledge-service, tests and gallery screens. The original 02-15 was split three ways to stay within scope: this plan (run lifecycle), 02-23 (source chips, evidence and history popovers) and 02-24 (review rows, corrections, rejected group, undo).
</objective>
