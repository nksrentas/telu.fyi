## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Give canonical meetings their transcript back: summaries of evidence-backed meetings are generated from finalized evidence (D-10), and the evidence transcript drawer — virtualized, highlight-capable, with a read-only legacy fallback — is ready for the living-doc page (02-14) to mount.

Purpose: Canonical meetings write only `transcript_evidence`, while the old summary input reads the legacy table (RESEARCH Pitfall 9). KNOW-02 needs evidence to open "in context"; the drawer is that context.
Output: Evidence-first `fetchAllTranscripts`, `TranscriptDrawer` with `highlight` and `scrollToEvidence`, and their tests. Split from the original 02-14 so the page plan stays within scope; this plan runs in Wave 3 beside the re-skins and adds no wave to the critical path.
</objective>
