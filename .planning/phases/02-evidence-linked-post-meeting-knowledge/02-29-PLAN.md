## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Close verification gap 2 (KNOW-06 / SC4, review CR-01): owner and due values must be shown as quoted and supported only when the transcript states them as whole words in evidence tied to the claim. Fold in WR-09 (a stated attribute never stores the model's own text) and WR-01 (claim quotes may not start or end mid-word), which use the same whole-token helper.

Purpose: The 02-03 prohibition "MUST NOT attribute an owner or due date that the quoted words do not state" is violated today, and a fabricated owner becomes eligible for Phase 3 after one Accept.
Output: `find_whole_token` in span_verifier.rs used by attribute verification, stated-text slicing and claim-quote matching; claim-tied attribute spans; unit and end-to-end regression tests.
</objective>
