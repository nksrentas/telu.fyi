## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>

Purpose: The exit gate for Phase 3 must be able to fail on every invariant and on missing inputs; today deletion residue is never measured, null leaves pass, and missing predictions inflate scores.
Output: Changes to `evaluation/mod.rs`, `evaluation/replay.rs` and `evaluation/tests.rs`. The CLI binary keeps compiling unchanged (its strictness and the thresholds file switch are 02-31).

No dependency on 02-29 (same wave): the gate tests here score hand-built predictions and labels (tests.rs `predicted`, `label`), and the replay and export tests assert only resurrection and deletion-residue counts, which do not depend on owner or due support, over whole-word verbatim claim quotes, so 02-29's attribute and whole-token rules change no expected value; the two plans modify no common file.
</objective>
