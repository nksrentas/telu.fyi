## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Let the user delete a meeting from its page with the counts shown first, and guarantee in one transaction that nothing derived from it survives.

Purpose: KNOW-08 and the one-way D-17/D-18 decisions; leftover apparently-supported knowledge is a roadmap stop condition.
Output: Explicit child-first deletion with a generic residue test, active-run cancellation, and the counts-first delete Alert wired into the doc toolbar.
</objective>
