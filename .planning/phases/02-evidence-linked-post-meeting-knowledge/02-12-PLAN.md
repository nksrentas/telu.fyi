## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Make regeneration safe: every new run matches its candidates against existing reviewed items so rejected and superseded knowledge never comes back, accepted items are reconfirmed instead of duplicated, and every suppression is auditable.

Purpose: KNOW-05 and D-11; a rejected item resurfacing is a roadmap stop condition.
Output: `matcher.rs`, suppressions migration, matcher-wired extraction and a resurrection invariant test.
</objective>
