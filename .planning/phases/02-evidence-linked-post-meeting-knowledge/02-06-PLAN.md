**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

Purpose: After 02-04, `accent` means Telu Indigo; untouched shadcn hover and focus styles would turn rows solid indigo and animate menus. D-01 needs these primitives right before the shell and living doc are built on them.
Output: Twelve restyled `components/ui` primitives with unchanged public APIs.
</objective>
