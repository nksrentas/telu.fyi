## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
First extraction lever for the KNOW-07 gap (02-VERIFICATION.md, SC5). The 02-37 diagnosis: about 7 items per 35-minute meeting against about 21 gold items, because the whole meeting goes to the 4B model in one completion; claims that assert details outside their quotes; proposals and stale recaps presented as decisions; answered questions; and commitments missing an owner or due date the meeting states.

Purpose: change the base extraction (the prompt, the window size and one missed-items pass) and measure it on the development set with the 02-38 tool, before the support and follow-up checks in 02-41 are layered on top.
Output: knowledge-extract-v3, capped windows, the missed-items pass (kept or removed by measurement), and a labeled development measurement in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
