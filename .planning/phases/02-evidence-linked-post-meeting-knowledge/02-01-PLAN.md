## Phase Goal

**As a** Telu user, **I want to** turn a completed meeting into trustworthy typed knowledge, inspect its exact evidence, and control what becomes eligible for future use, **so that** only reviewed knowledge I can trace to the exact transcript words is carried into later meetings.

<objective>
Prove the phase architecture end-to-end on one path: a user presses Find knowledge on a completed meeting, Rust reads its finalized evidence, dispatches one extraction through the revalidated Summary route, verifies every quote verbatim against its labeled segment, persists an append-only run with its candidates, and the meeting page lists each candidate with its exact quote.

Purpose: Validate the one-way ledger contract (D-08), the evidence rule (D-15) and route reuse (D-09) on one real slice before any expansion is layered on top.
Output: Ledger migration, Rust `knowledge` module (models, repository, verifier, extraction, commands), shared Summary dispatch resolver, evidence read path, TS service, and a Knowledge section mounted on the existing meeting page.
</objective>
