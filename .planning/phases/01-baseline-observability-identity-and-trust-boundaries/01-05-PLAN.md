## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Native capture → transcription queue | Latency-sensitive work is accepted or rejected without blocking capture. |
| Worker → queue ledger | Terminal outcomes determine later completeness. |

## STRIDE Threat Register (ASVS L1; High blocks)

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-05-01 | Denial of service | transcription queues | high | mitigate | Finite channels, non-blocking admission, explicit rejection/gaps, overload tests. |
| T-05-02 | Tampering / Repudiation | work outcomes | high | mitigate | One terminal outcome per accepted ID plus conservation invariants/reasons. |
| T-05-03 | Information disclosure | ledger snapshots | medium | mitigate | Counts/IDs/redacted codes only; no transcript/audio content. |
</threat_model>
