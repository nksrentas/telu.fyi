## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Provider config → Rust resolver | Endpoints/brands are untrusted until parsed and classified. |
| Rust route contract → dispatch | Used destination must match validated route. |

## STRIDE Threat Register (ASVS L1; High blocks)

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-10-01 | Spoofing / Information disclosure | custom/Ollama classification | high | mitigate | Parse/resolve/sanitize and fail on material change. |
| T-10-02 | Tampering | provider fallback | high | mitigate | Remove substitution and bind dispatch to confirmed route/provider. |
| T-10-03 | Information disclosure | route logs/errors | high | mitigate | Sanitized origin only; strip credentials/query/fragment/path. |
| T-10-04 | Repudiation | shown versus used route | high | mitigate | Validate immediately before dispatch and snapshot per meeting/run. |
</threat_model>
