# Sketch 005: Conversation Mental Models

## Design Question

Which product-level mental model lets people prepare, listen, review, and carry context forward without navigating between disconnected feature screens or sacrificing useful desktop space?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/005-conversation-mental-models/index.html`.

## Variants

- **A: Meeting Continuum** — Every meeting is one persistent workspace with an explicit Prepare → Listen live → Review → Carry forward journey.
- **B: Day Stream** — Time is the navigation. Meetings expand, become live, and resolve into outcomes inside one chronological day.
- **C: Conversation Spaces** — Recurring groups and relationships are persistent places containing their current meeting, people, unresolved threads, and evidence-backed memory.

## Interactions

- Variant A: switch lifecycle stages, begin the meeting, and finish into review.
- Variant B: begin and finish the 11:00 meeting without leaving the day stream.
- Variant C: enter the Studio team live room or switch conversation spaces.
- Evidence controls open an origin-aware provenance popover.
- The utility toolbar previews desktop, tablet, and phone layouts.

## What to Look For

Judge how quickly each direction answers three questions: Where am I? What should I do now? Where will this information go next? Also compare how much useful information remains visible without creating dashboard noise.
