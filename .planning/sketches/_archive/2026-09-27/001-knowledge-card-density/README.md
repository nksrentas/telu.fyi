# Sketch 001: Knowledge Card Density

## Design Question

What information lets a user judge a candidate quickly without turning the Knowledge pane into a wall of metadata?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/001-knowledge-card-density/index.html`.

## Variants

- **A: Signal First** — Claim, state, unresolved markers, evidence chips, and review actions; details expand inline.
- **B: Evidence Preview** — Adds a short supporting quote to every resting card.
- **C: Review Rows** — Dense rows drive a persistent focused-detail panel.

## What to Look For

Compare scan speed, confidence before opening an item, and whether the evidence preview earns the extra vertical space. Click evidence chips and review actions; use the viewport controls to compare desktop and mobile widths.
