# Sketch 002: Knowledge Card Expansion

## Design Question

How should Telu reveal evidence, structured fields, provenance, and revision history while preserving the user's place in the grouped review list?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/002-knowledge-card-expansion/index.html`.

## Variants

- **A: Inline Accordion** — Expands the selected card in place and keeps the transcript visible.
- **B: Review Sheet** — Opens a right-side inspection surface over the Knowledge pane.
- **C: Focused Dialog** — Centers one candidate and its evidence in a modal review step.

## What to Look For

Compare context preservation, room for correction and history, and how easily the user can move to the next candidate. Every “Review details” control opens the corresponding pattern.
