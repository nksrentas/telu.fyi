# Sketch 003: Beautiful Knowledge Sections

## Design Question

How should decisions, commitments, and open questions become visually distinct sections while evidence and review remain immediate?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/003-beautiful-knowledge-sections/index.html`.

## Variants

- **A: Editorial Ledger** — A composed document-like ledger with numbered statements and a quiet evidence inspector.
- **B: Sculpted Sections** — Each knowledge type has its own spatial treatment: decision band, commitment timeline, and question field.
- **C: Focused Review** — One generous review surface at the top, with compact type sections acting as the queue beneath it.

## What to Look For

Judge the beauty of the overall composition, how quickly the eye distinguishes knowledge types, and whether the selected-item evidence feels trustworthy without becoming technical. Click any item, inspect its transcript evidence, and use Accept/Correct/Reject to feel the review state change.
