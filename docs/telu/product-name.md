# Product name

## Decision

The product name is **Telu**, styled as **telu** in the product wordmark and most brand contexts.

The intended domain is **telu.fyi**.

## Why it fits

Telu is short, memorable, easy to pronounce, and broad enough for the product to grow beyond meeting transcription. It has a subtle “tell you” association: the product quietly tells the user what is relevant before, during, and after a conversation without sounding literal or overly descriptive.

That association supports the intended product behavior:

- Before a meeting, Telu tells the user what context matters.
- During a meeting, Telu surfaces useful questions or facts at the right moment.
- After a meeting, Telu preserves what was decided, what remains open, and what may have been missed.

The name should communicate a calm, useful intelligence rather than a loud or anthropomorphic AI assistant.

## Naming conventions

- Product name in prose: **Telu**
- Wordmark and UI brand styling: **telu**
- Repository folder: `telu-fyi`
- Domain direction: `telu.fyi`
- Avoid expanding Telu into a forced acronym.
- Avoid appending “AI” to the product name unless needed for clarity in descriptive copy.

## Domain note

The naming conversation identified `telu.fyi` as the preferred domain and found it apparently available at the time. Availability and registrar pricing can change, so both must be verified immediately before registration.
